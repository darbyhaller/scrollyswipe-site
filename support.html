Frequently asked

Is this real money?

No. ScrollySwipe is a play-money prediction game. Your balance is virtual — you can't deposit, withdraw, or cash out. It's just for fun and bragging rights.

How do I get more play money?

You earn an automatic salary every hour just for showing up. Win predictions to grow your portfolio faster.

How are markets resolved?

Crypto markets resolve based on the live Coinbase price at the moment the timer hits zero. If the price is above the strike, OVER wins; otherwise UNDER wins.

How do I delete my account?

Email darbyhallerapps@gmail.com from the address tied to your account and we'll wipe it within 7 days.
