The Basics

What is ScrollySwipe, and how does it work?

🎯 Predict & Profit

ScrollySwipe is a real-time prediction market. Each card shows a YES or NO question with a countdown timer — you bet YES if you think the answer will be yes when the timer runs out, or NO if you think it won't.

⏱️ Fast Markets

Markets expire quickly — as short as 5 minutes. When a market resolves, you get paid out instantly if you were right, and a fresh market takes its place. There's always something to bet on.

💰 Play Money

You start with $100 in play money. No real money involved — just bragging rights and the thrill of the game. Plus you earn a salary of $10 every 30 seconds, so you're never stuck.

How Pricing Works

Prices reflect what the crowd believes. Every bet moves the market.

📊 Reading the Prices

Each market shows two prices that always add up to $1.00:

YES 72¢
NO 28¢
  • A YES price of 72¢ means the market thinks there's roughly a 72% chance the outcome is YES
  • If you bet YES at 72¢ and win, your shares pay out at $1 — a gain of 28¢ per share
  • If you bet NO at 28¢ and win, your shares pay out at $1 — a gain of 72¢ per share
  • Lower prices = higher risk, higher reward

⚖️ Automated Market Maker

Prices are set by an LMSR (Logarithmic Market Scoring Rule) — a mathematical formula that provides instant liquidity. There's no waiting for someone to take the other side of your bet. Key properties:

  • Every bet you place moves the price — buying YES makes YES more expensive
  • Large bets move the price more than small ones
  • You can always buy or sell at any time — there's always a price
  • If you think the price is wrong, you can profit by betting against it
Pro tip: If the market says YES is 90¢ but you think there's a real chance of NO, the 10¢ NO bet pays 10x if you're right. Look for mispriced markets.

Positions & Selling

You don't have to wait for resolution — you can sell anytime.

📈 Your Position

When you have an open position in a market, you'll see it displayed on the card:

  • Your side (YES or NO) and number of shares
  • Current unrealized value of your position
  • P&L (profit/loss) with a trend arrow and sparkline graph
  • The sparkline updates live as the price moves

🔄 Auto-Sell on Flip

If you hold YES shares and then swipe NO (or vice versa), your existing position is automatically sold first before opening the new one. You don't have to manually close positions.

  • Hold 10 YES, bet 15 NO → sells 10 YES, opens 5 NO
  • Hold 5 NO, bet 3 YES → sells 3 NO, position reduced to 2 NO
  • This makes it fast to change your mind mid-market
Pro tip: If you bet YES early at 50¢ and the price rises to 80¢, you can sell by swiping NO to lock in profit before the market resolves. Don't be greedy — take profits.

The HUD

Your at-a-glance dashboard, always visible at the top.

🎛️ HUD Elements

  • 💲 Balance (top-left) — Your current play money. Shows green ▲ when increasing, red ▼ when decreasing. Tap it to open the bet size picker.
  • Bet size (next to balance) — The amount you're betting per swipe/tap, shown in yellow. Tap to choose between 3 preset sizes.
  • 📊 Positions (top-right) — Number of your currently open positions across all markets.

🎚️ Bet Sizing

The app offers 3 bet sizes that scale with your balance. The largest option is roughly 5% of your balance — enough to be meaningful without being reckless.

  • Small balance (e.g. $100): choices might be 1, 2, 5
  • Medium balance (e.g. $1,000): choices might be 10, 20, 50
  • Large balance (e.g. $10,000): choices might be 100, 200, 500

Pro Tips

Strategies for climbing the leaderboard.

Start Small

Use the lowest bet size while you learn how prices move. The salary keeps you funded, so there's no rush.

Watch the Momentum

If something is trending hard in one direction, the 5-minute market is more predictable than you think. Ride the trend.

Buy the Underdog

Markets at 90/10 have poor risk-reward on the favorite side. Look for 60/40 or 70/30 markets where you have an edge.

Sell Before Resolution

You don't have to hold until expiry. If the price moved in your favor, swipe the opposite direction to lock in profit early.

Diversify Durations

Mix 5-minute plays (quick trades) with 24-hour positions (high-conviction calls) to balance risk and reward.

Read the Card

Each card shows you the current state of the market. If the odds are close to 50/50, it's a toss-up. If one side is heavily favored, ask yourself if the crowd is wrong.
